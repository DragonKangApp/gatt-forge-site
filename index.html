Frequently asked questions

No devices?

Use a compatible BLE peripheral that is advertising nearby. Enable Bluetooth, allow permission, clear filters and scan again. This tool does not find every Classic Bluetooth device.

Missing Read/Write/Notify?

Actions depend on GATT properties. Pending reads or notification changes temporarily block conflicting operations to keep RX classification reliable.

After Bluetooth reset/disconnection?

Scan and reconnect. Return from expired GATT details and discover current services again.

Send a saved command?

Connect in Devices, then choose the command and a matching characteristic in Commands. Matching UUIDs do not guarantee that the payload matches your device protocol.

Free versus Pro?

Free includes core BLE, timeline search/filters and basic tools, the latest 10 completed sessions and up to 10 commands. One-time Pro unlocks all history, unlimited commands and TXT/JSON/CSV export. Entitlement changes do not automatically delete old data.

Purchased but locked?

Use the original purchasing Apple Account and select Restore Purchases on the Pro page. Pending or unverified transactions do not unlock early. Contact support if needed; refund requests go through Apple's official channels.

Are logs uploaded?

The app does not automatically upload them to the developer. Sharing you initiate, device backups and Apple's purchase services are separate; see the privacy policy.

Continuous background recording?

Not promised. The app is a foreground tool and performs only bounded asynchronous saving in the background. Force quit or expired background time can lose uncommitted tail events.

Why does large history load?

Events load in segments on demand. Export is disabled until complete. Missing/damaged files produce an error and are preserved rather than silently exporting partial history.

Delete all data?

Individual records can be deleted in the app. Migration backups and temporary exports may remain in its sandbox. Deleting the app removes that local sandbox; backups and already shared files require separate management.

Why does a connection fail?

Check that the peripheral is nearby, accepts connections and is not occupied by another central device. End other connections, then scan, connect and discover services again. Follow the peripheral documentation for pairing or reset requirements.

What happens after a refund?

When Apple revokes the verified Pro entitlement, Free limits apply and export locks again. Existing sessions and commands are not automatically deleted. Apple handles refund requests and decisions.

Why can’t I export?

TXT / JSON / CSV export requires a valid Pro entitlement and fully loaded session events. Unreadable or damaged history is not silently exported as a partial file. If saving or sharing fails, check the destination service and available system storage; record the error for support.
