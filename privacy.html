On-device processing

GATT Forge is a BLE development and debugging tool. With Bluetooth permission, it processes nearby peripheral names, system-provided identifiers, signal strength and advertisement information on your device. Connection sessions record device information, timestamps, GATT identifiers, read/write/notification payloads, errors and operation logs. Saved commands include names, groups, UUIDs, payloads and write types. A local preference stores timeline ordering.

This data supports device inspection, debugging, history and command reuse. The current version has no advertising, analytics or tracking SDK, no app account and no developer-operated server. It does not automatically send these logs to the developer.

Purchases and restoration

Optional lifetime Pro is purchased through Apple StoreKit. Apple handles payment and Apple Account information; the app uses verified purchase entitlements supplied by Apple to enable features. We do not receive your payment card information or Apple Account password. Apple's own privacy policy applies to its services.

Sharing and copying that you initiate

Exports and sharing are initiated by you. An export may include device names, identifiers, raw payloads and debugging information. Review it before sharing. A mail, file or other sharing service you select receives the file according to your action and its own policies. Copy places selected content on the system clipboard.

Retention and deletion

Sessions and commands stay in the app's local storage until you delete records or delete the app. The free plan limits access to older history; it does not automatically erase it. Legacy migration backups are retained for recovery; deleting an individual session does not also remove that backup. Temporary exports may remain until the system clears them or the app is deleted. You manage copies already shared outside the app.

Deleting the app removes its local sandbox data, but system backups and exported copies may remain and must be managed separately. There is no app-operated cloud sync; device backups may include app data depending on your system settings.

Permission and choices

You can revoke Bluetooth access in system settings. This prevents Bluetooth scanning and connections without automatically deleting history. No app registration is required. The app does not track you for advertising.
